How I work

Every project is different. The method adapts to the problem, not the other way round.

From idea to delivery
01

Discover

Talk to the people doing the work. Map the real process, not the official one.

02

Decide

Turn findings into architecture, priorities and user stories.

03

Design

Set the framework, lead the design team, prototype until it works.

04

Deliver

Hand over to development and stay close until it ships: fix, document, measure, evolve.

AI in my process

AI does the heavy lifting. I make the calls.

I use it to read, map and draft faster, so more of my time goes into the product decisions.

Client data stays anonymised. Every output is reviewed before it reaches a client or a developer.

  • User research30+ interviews for a consultancy’s new branding and website, transcribed, tagged and synthesised in Dovetail, then turned into the executive summary that defended the research and the first design decisions with the board. 60% less time.
  • From tender to backlogTenders and reports turned into AS-IS and TO-BE flows, then epics, user stories and acceptance criteria in Jira, with documentation in Confluence. A 130-page tender analysed in a few hours, not 2 weeks.
  • Design system auditsThe Services Portal’s design system, inherited from another agency, audited with Claude connected to Figma through MCP: ~15% inconsistencies, from missing button states to typography without variables. 70% less time from audit to a consolidated system.
  • Prototypes in codeWorking prototypes with Lovable and Claude Code to test real interactions before detailed design. This portfolio was built the same way.

About
